    05

    How this was measured

    Topics. topics in six areas: health, farming and food, government schemes and rights, science and environment, money and work, plus the 300 diseases with an ICD-10 code that the most Wikipedias write about (found with a Wikidata SPARQL query).

    Does an article exist? From Wikidata, which links every topic to its article in each language.

    How complete is it? Article size compared with the English article. Indian scripts take about three bytes per letter (Urdu, Sindhi and Kashmiri about two), so sizes are converted to letters first. Below 15% of the English length counts as a stub, below 50% as partial, and anything more as good.

    What do people want to read? Twelve months of English Wikipedia page views for each topic (). The Knowledge Access Score weights each topic by these views.

    Limits. Length is only a rough stand-in for quality. A long article can be out of date and a short one can be excellent. English page views reflect readers worldwide, not only in India. Speaker numbers are approximate first-language figures from the 2011 Census, and Bhojpuri speakers are counted under Hindi in the Census.
